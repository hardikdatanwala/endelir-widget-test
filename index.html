Endelir chat button test

A plain page on a site that is not endelir.com, used to check that the Endelir chat button works on a business's own website: the button opens, a chat starts, and a reply arrives.

The button is the one line every business pastes into their site:

<script async src="https://chat.endelir.com/_chat/w.js" data-page="endelir-team/talk-to-us"></script>

Look for Chat with us in the bottom-right corner.
